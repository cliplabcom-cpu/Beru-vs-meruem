# Module 03 — Prompts for a specialist image tool (optional)

Why this file exists: I cannot produce final 3D-cartoon art. The renders here are layout/scale/palette references. If you want finished-looking environment art from an image-generation tool, these prompts carry the identity. **I have not tested them in any tool, and I can't promise a given tool will keep the same environment across images.** Use the `*_main` PNG as the reference image if the tool accepts one. Check each tool's current free limits and export size yourself before relying on it.

Shared style line (put at the start of every prompt):
> Simplified 3D cartoon environment, clean chunky shapes, soft flat lighting from the upper left, muted warm palette (cream, plaster, sand, clay, wood, sage, slate), matte materials, no people, no text or letters anywhere, uncluttered, vertical 9:16 composition.

**A – City street.** `<style line>` Calm two-storey plaster shopfronts with striped awnings, wide sidewalk, glass bus shelter with a wooden bench, a green clock post, four lamps, one tree in a planter, a gap between shops with bunting, pale morning sky. Signs and screens are blank.

**B – Asha's café.** `<style line>` Small warm café interior, wooden service counter with an espresso machine on the left, a glass pastry case in the centre, a register on the right, a blank dark chalkboard on the wall, two wall shelves with glass jars and one plant, round wooden tables, checkered cream floor, windows on the left wall, a few warm pendant lamps, a small red notebook on the counter.

**C – Market stall.** `<style line>` Open plaza with a plaster building behind, string bunting in clay, sage and cream, one sage-and-cream striped market stall with a wooden counter, two-tier display of round baked snacks on white plates, two tins, a blank dark price board, a phone stand with a blank pay plate, a small red notebook.

**D – City bus interior.** `<style line>` Inside a city bus, long aisle, sage green seats in rows, deep sage overhead handrails, cream ceiling with warm strip lights, large windows both sides, blank dark destination panel at the front, driver's seat with a steering wheel and a thermos.

**Negative prompts / avoid:** photorealistic, cinematic movie look, extra clutter, readable text, logos, QR codes, people, harsh black shadows, neon colours.

**Consistency tip:** generate the main frame of each environment first, then use that image as the reference for every other angle of the same environment.
