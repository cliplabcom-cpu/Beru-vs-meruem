# Module 03 – Environment Consistency Notes (v2)

Applies to the 14 corrected references in this folder. Supplements (does not replace) `03_Global_Visual_Consistency_Guide.md` and `03_Environment_Design_Guide.md`.

## Shared rules (verified in the renders)
- **Palette:** cream / sand / clay / brown / sage / slate-blue; no saturated accents except small intentional ones (red tray, teal screens, gold sign band in Year 10).
- **Key light:** upper-left-front. Warm lit faces, cool soft shadows, blurred contact shadows under every object that touches the ground; mild vignette/light falloff in post (`kit.grade`).
- **Style:** flat-shaded simplified 3D, 2x supersampled; no photoreal textures. Materials are suggested only by tone breaks (wood panel seams, glass streaks, pale metal highlights).
- **Scale (1 m = 300 px basis):** Emeka 1.85 m (100 %), Sam 1.76 m (95 %), Asha 1.63 m (88 %). Shop door 2.10, café counter 1.05, market counter 1.00, bus ceiling 2.30, handrail 2.00, seat back 1.13, bus aisle 1.06.
- **Cast placement rule:** never all three in the foreground; no character covers the counter, doorway, machine/terminal or a focal prop; characters never overlap each other in the placement tests.

## Character cut-out limits (important for Phase 4)
Characters in v2 are cut-outs of the APPROVED front / side / back views, scaled to metres. They are **placement and scale references, not acting poses**. Each is a flat card (no turnaround). Real animation needs the specialist character pipeline.

## Known geometric fact
Approved front views are ~1.0 m (Emeka) / ~0.7 m (Sam) / ~0.8 m (Asha) wide with arms. The bus aisle is 1.06 m, so two characters cannot stand abreast in the aisle in a front view. D4 therefore uses profile views for Sam and Emeka at the front (Emeka in the door vestibule), and Asha in the aisle.
