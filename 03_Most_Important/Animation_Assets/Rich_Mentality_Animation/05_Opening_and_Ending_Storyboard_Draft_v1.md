## 1. The key finding before the concepts

**The suggested ending message overlaps what S62–S63 already say.** "Ambition is powerful… without purpose it's an endless chase" is the same idea as S62–63. Adding it again would repeat the film, and the brief itself says not to summarise.

**So the two scenes do different jobs:**
- **Opening:** ask the question and say what it is not ("not a fortune"). It plants the idea that S5 later pays off.
- **Main video:** shows the consequences. S62–63 give the warning about ambition.
- **Ending:** answers the opening question about wealth. It uses new imagery and a **different** line from S62–63. It is short.

## 2. Opening concept — "First light"

**Idea:** the film starts on the **same A1 camera** as shot 1, a few minutes earlier. It is pre-dawn, empty and cool, with one warm window. The narrator asks the question. The light then warms into the exact grade of shot 1.

- **Why this works:** it establishes the world with no new assets. It avoids money and luxury imagery. The question is carried by voice and on-screen text. The handoff to S1 is a colour change, not an effect.
- **Window:** the one lit window is the café. That is where Asha works, and it is a quiet nod to S7 (5:40 a.m.). The brief asks for a continuous world, and this gives one anchor point. **[verify on plate: which A1 shopfront is the café.]**
- **Hook text:** the question appears large in the middle band. "Rich mentality" gets a **gold pen-stroke underline that draws itself**. That echoes Asha's notebook pen-draw (S15), not any money symbol.

## 3. Ending concept — "Numbers and names"

**Idea:** a mirror of the opening. The same camera is at **dusk** now, at Year 10, with many windows lit from within. Asha has a quiet moment holding her notebook. The last page we see holds **names**, drawn as abstract scribble lines, not sums. That echoes S15's bars and icons. The narrator's closing line contrasts a number with names, and the notebook closes into the Rich Mentality mark.

- **Why this works:** the meaning arrives through an object we already know. It is not a summary or a lecture. Dusk is the story reason for the one deviation from "no dusk without a reason" in the consistency guide: it is the bookend to dawn.
- **Bookend pairs:** dawn ↔ dusk, one lit window ↔ many lit windows, empty street ↔ street in use, the question ↔ the answer, sums ↔ names.

## 5. Storyboard

Format 1080×1920, 24 fps. **Opening 8.2 s. Ending 16.5 s. Total added 24.7 s, so the film becomes about 204.7 s (≈ 3:25).** "Plate" means an existing approved environment image. "Flat insert" means a flat 2D graphic in the project palette, like S15 and S62.

### OPENING

#### O1 — First light (0.0–5.2 s)
- **Visual:** A1 plate in a pre-dawn grade: desaturated blue-grey and warm-grey, **not teal** (teal is reserved for competition). The street is empty and the lamps are on. One café window glows warm. The first frame is already a complete image; there is no fade from black.
- **Camera:** locked, very slow push-in (no more than 104%). It matches the S1 camera.
- **Characters:** none.
- **Narration / text:** voice from 0.4 s: "What if, overnight, everyone in the world developed a rich mentality?" Text appears from about 1.2 s. The gold underline draws under "rich mentality" at about 3.4 s.
- **Sound:** near-silence for the first 0.3 s. Then a cold air bed, a distant idling bus, and a shutter rising far off. The four lamps click off in a stagger from about 4.2 s.
- **Transition:** continuous into O2 with no cut.
- **Complexity:** Low–Medium. Needs a grade, a window glow overlay and lamp glow overlays. **[verify on plate]**

#### O2 — Warming (5.2–8.2 s)
- **Visual:** the same plate. The grade ramps from pre-dawn to the exact warm-neutral morning grade of S1. Lamp glow is gone, the window glow softens, and the sky brightens.
- **Camera:** locked, same push continues.
- **Characters:** none.
- **Narration / text:** "Not a fortune. A way of thinking." The underline fades. Text is gone by 7.6 s.
- **Sound:** the bus rumble rises, a bird or two (synth). The bed crossfades into S1's street bed so there is no jump at the cut.
- **Transition:** **hard cut into S1.** The street goes from empty to populated, which reads as Day 1 beginning. Check in the animatic.
- **Complexity:** Low.

### ENDING

#### E1 — Breath (S63 fade, then 1.0 s)
- **Visual:** the S63 fade to black is kept as it is. Then 1.0 s of black.
- **Camera / characters:** none.
- **Narration:** none.
- **Sound:** S63's last 0.3 s is already silent. A quiet evening street bed fades up under the black.
- **Transition:** soft 0.5 s fade up into E2.
- **Complexity:** Low.

#### E2 — The street at dusk (3.5 s)
- **Visual:** A3 plate in a dusk grade: warm gold light, lamps lit, windows glowing, the outdoor café table. Flat silhouettes walk home. One option: two silhouettes carry a crate between them across the foreground, which quietly says "carried together." A bus passes by sound only. A3 has no bus. **[verify on plate: lamps and windows]**
- **Camera:** locked, same camera as O1 and S1, slow push (no more than 104%).
- **Characters:** flat silhouettes only.
- **Narration:** none. This is the breathing space.
- **Sound:** soft sparse music enters very low (licensed or royalty-free, to be sourced, licence logged). Street bed, an engine passing.
- **Transition:** hard cut.
- **Complexity:** Medium. Dusk grade plus silhouettes. The paired crate walk is the untested part. Fallback: one silhouette holds a door for another.

#### E3 — A number (5.0 s)
- **Visual:** C1 plaza in the same dusk grade. The sage-and-cream stall is closed behind her. **Asha stands in front, jacket only, red notebook in her left hand, front view, expression Satisfied.** A small optional touch: the **terracotta-satchel silhouette** (the first customer from S24–25) passes behind her. It is only an Easter egg.
- **Camera:** locked, very slight push.
- **Characters:** Asha holds, with breathing and a blink-hidden swap only.
- **Narration:** from 0.4 s, "Ask someone what they own, and they'll give you a number." (Option R.)
- **Sound:** plaza dusk bed, music under the voice (about 15–20 dB below). Footsteps if the satchel figure walks.
- **Transition:** hard cut to the notebook.
- **Complexity:** Medium. **Depends on the neutral no-apron Asha art.** See section 7.

#### E4 — Names (4.0 s)
- **Visual:** flat insert on a wood counter. The red notebook opens to a page of **names**: short, uneven scribble lines, each with a small dot beside it. No readable letters. The pen adds one more line. At 3.2 s the cover swings shut.
- **Camera:** locked close.
- **Characters:** none.
- **Narration:** from 0.3 s, "Ask what they've built, and you'll hear names."
- **Sound:** pen scratch, a paper rustle, the cover closing. Music holds. After the line, 0.5 s of near-silence.
- **Transition:** the closed notebook stays in place and becomes the mark in E5.
- **Complexity:** Low.

#### E5 — The mark (3.0 s)
- **Visual:** the closed red notebook (**#C8493A**) with a cream tape strip and a small gold coin (**#D9B25F**) taped to the cover. It eases up and shrinks slightly on the same wood-and-dusk background. **"Rich Mentality" wordmark fades in beneath it, added in the editor.** No tagline. The film holds and fades out.
- **Camera:** locked.
- **Characters:** none.
- **Narration:** none.
- **Sound:** one soft coin-on-wood tick as the wordmark lands. That is the same sound as S2, S25 and S56. The music rings out and the film ends in 0.5 s of near-silence.
- **Transition:** end.
- **Complexity:** Low. Flat graphic plus editor type.

## 8. Honest critique

1. **The hook is quiet.** A calm dawn street with a question is gentler than most hooks, and it may lose viewers in the first 3 seconds. Mitigations are the lit window, the lamps clicking off, and the pen-draw underline. An alternative is to open inside the café at 5:40 with the notebook, but that repeats S7. **I would test the hook in the animatic against that alternative before locking.**
2. **Runtime.** The film grows to about 3:25. The earlier lean cut (S44 and S46, about 4 s) is still available. The ending could drop the satchel passer and the crate walk.
3. **Four aphorisms in a row** (S62, S63, then two ending lines). The 1.0 s black breath helps. Option R deliberately uses concrete imagery, not slogans.
4. **"Who you become" is mostly visual** in option R. Asha's face carries it. If you want it spoken, choose B or edit R.
5. **The ending has one lead, Asha.** Emeka and Sam closed their arcs in S56–61. A shared three-person frame is untested and I did not use one.
6. **The identity mark is my invention.** It is built from existing in-world objects, but it is a new design that needs your approval.
7. **Everything above is untested.** No animation, grade, voice, music or timing has been run.
